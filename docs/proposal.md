# Website Proposal: Yorlin Cleaning, LLC

For assignment topic 1, I will build and publish a simple website for Yorlin Cleaning, LLC, so customers in La Crosse County can find us, see our services, and book a cleaning online. Yorlin Cleaning is a registered and insured residential and commercial cleaning company.

## The Problem

Without a website, customers cannot easily find us, see what we offer, or book without calling or sending a message. That costs the business in three ways:

- **Lost customers.** People search online before they hire. If we don't show up, they choose a competitor.
- **Weak credibility.** A business with no site, or an old one, looks less established.
- **Wasted time.** I answer the same questions about prices, hours, and services by hand.

## Who Will Use It

- **Customers:** busy families, people moving in or out, and local businesses in La Crosse County. Most will visit on their phones to see services, check prices, and book.
- **Me and my team:** we keep the site current, manage bookings, and send customers to it instead of repeating answers.

## What I Will Build

A clean website that works on phones and computers, with five pages:

| Page | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Shows what we do and who we serve, with one clear "Book Now" button |
| Services | Residential, move-in/move-out, deep cleaning, and regular maintenance, each with a starting price |
| Booking | Customers pick a date and time; a confirmation goes to them and to me |
| Gallery | Photos of our work and customer reviews |
| About & Contact | Our story, phone (608-386-7651), email (yorlincleaning@gmail.com), hours, service-area map, and a contact form |

The design will use our pink, rose-gold, and black brand colors, our logo, and our tagline, "Clean Spaces Happy Lives." It will show that we are a registered and insured LLC.

## Timeline

The site will be live in 4 weeks at yorlincleaning.com (or the closest available domain).

1. **Week 1:** Gather the logo, photos, service list, and prices.
2. **Week 2:** Build the Home, Services, and About & Contact pages.
3. **Week 3:** Add Booking and Gallery.
4. **Week 4:** Test on a phone and a laptop, fix problems, and publish.

## How I Will Show It

The showcase has three parts:

1. **The working website.** A five-page site, shown live or on screen.
2. **An architecture diagram.** One picture that follows a customer from a search to a confirmed booking, so someone new to the topic can see how the pieces fit.
3. **A walkthrough and cost comparison.** I will book a cleaning step by step, then compare the cost of building the site myself, using a website builder, and hiring a freelancer.

The presentation will run 5 to 7 minutes.

## Why It Matters

- **It works around the clock.** It answers questions, takes bookings, and builds trust even when I am busy.
- **More customers without more ad spend,** and fewer interruptions for routine questions.
- **I own it.** Unlike a social media page, its rules and reach cannot change on me.
- **I can measure it.** Monthly visits and bookings show what to improve.

## Success

10 new booking requests per month through the website within three months of launch.

> Note: the built site differs from this plan in three ways. Booking is a request form that the owner confirms (a live calendar needs a paid service). The map is left out to avoid third-party tracking. Prices, hours, photos, and reviews are not shown yet, because they are added only when real.
