# Cost Analysis & Reasoning: Yorlin Cleaning Website

My website costs about $10.46 in its first year, because I built it myself and chose free hosting. A website builder would cost roughly $200 to $350 a year, and hiring a freelancer roughly $1,500 to $5,000 up front. I chose the lowest-cost option and accepted a few tradeoffs, explained below.

## What my website costs

| Cost item | What it is for | Estimated cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Domain name (.com)](https://www.whtop.com/plans/cloudflare.com/135912) | The web address, such as yorlincleaning.com | $10.46 per year |
| [Hosting (Cloudflare Pages, free plan)](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits) | Stores the pages and serves them over HTTPS | $0 |
| [Contact form (Formspree, free plan)](https://splitforms.com/formspree-pricing) | Delivers booking requests to email, up to 50 a month | $0 |
| Business email | Receives requests (my existing Gmail address) | $0 |
| Photos and content | Taken with a phone and written by me | $0 |
| Google Business Profile | Free listing that helps local search | $0 |
| My build time | Design, writing, and testing | $0 in cash |
| **Total, first year** | | **$10.46** |

After year one the cost stays at $10.46 a year, about $0.87 a month, as long as the free plans do not change. The free hosting plan allows up to 20,000 files and 500 builds a month. My site has fewer than 20 files, so I am far inside the limit.

## Three ways to get the same website

| Option | First-year cost | Cost over 3 years | Control | My effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Build it myself on free hosting (my choice) | $10.46 | about $31 | Full: I own every file and can change hosts | High |
| B. [Website builder](https://www.expertmarket.com/website-builders/wix-vs-squarespace) (Wix or Squarespace) | about $200 to $350 | about $610 to $1,040 | Limited: tied to the platform | Low |
| C. [Hire a freelancer](https://seahawkmedia.com/tech/small-business-website-costs/) | about $1,500 to $5,000, plus hosting and optional maintenance | about $1,640 and up | Depends on the contract | Lowest |

Option B uses list prices of $17 to $29 a month. Option C's three-year figure adds shared hosting at the low end ($3 a month) and a domain to the lowest freelancer price. Freelancer maintenance plans are extra, often $49 to $99 a month.

## Why I chose option A

- **Lowest cost.** A small business watches its cash closely. A $10.46 site leaves the rest for supplies and marketing.
- **Full ownership.** The site is a folder of plain files. I can move it to another host in minutes, and no platform can raise the price or lock the files in.
- **Less to maintain and secure.** The site has no database, plugins, or logins, so there is little to update and little to attack.
- **Fast and reliable.** Plain pages load quickly and are served from the host's global network.

## Tradeoffs I accepted

- **No live calendar.** Customers request a date and I confirm it. That costs a few minutes per booking, but avoids a $49 monthly plan or card fees.
- **Editing needs basic skills.** A builder is drag and drop. My go-live guide lists exactly where to change text, prices, and photos.
- **Free plans can change.** Because the files are portable, I could move to another free host without rebuilding.
- **My time is unpaid, but it is real.** Option A trades hours for dollars. That suits a student project and a tight budget. A busy owner with no spare time might reasonably choose option B.

## Does it pay for itself?

Assume a typical cleaning brings in $120. This is my assumption, so replace it with the real price. Then one booking covers the whole first year of option A, even with the email upgrade ($94.46). Option B takes about 2 to 3 bookings, and option C about 13 to 42. These figures count revenue, not profit, so the real break-even takes more jobs. My proposal's goal is 10 new booking requests a month, so if only one request a month becomes a job, option A pays for itself within the first month.
