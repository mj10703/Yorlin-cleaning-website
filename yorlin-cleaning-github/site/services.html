Cleaning services

Every space is different, so we confirm the details and the price with you before we book.

Residential cleaning

Regular and one-time cleaning for houses, apartments, and condos. We clean kitchens, bathrooms, floors, and the rooms in between.

Good for: Busy households, guests on the way, or a home that needs a fresh start.

Move-in / move-out cleaning

A full clean of an empty home, before you hand over the keys or before you unpack the first box.

Good for: Renters leaving a unit, new owners, and landlords between tenants.

Deep cleaning

Extra time and attention for buildup and the spots a regular clean skips.

Good for: A first visit, a seasonal reset, or a home that has gone a while between cleanings.

Regular maintenance

Recurring visits on a schedule that suits you, to keep a clean home clean.

Good for: Households that want steady upkeep without having to plan each visit.

Commercial cleaning

Cleaning for offices and other commercial spaces in La Crosse County. Call us to talk through your space.

Good for: Small offices and local businesses.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us about your space and we will help you choose. Booking is a request, not a commitment, until we confirm with you.
